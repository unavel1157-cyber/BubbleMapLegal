Privacy Policy

Bubble Map · Effective Date: November 15, 2026

Bubble Map ("the App") respects and protects users' privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when providing the App's services and how users can manage their data.

1. Information We Process

1.1 Location Information

When users create or move a place on the map, the App processes relevant information, including latitude and longitude, place names, original place names, addresses, and place record timestamps. This information is used to display and manage the map bubbles created by users and is stored locally on their devices using SwiftData.

1.2 Device Location Information

The App may request iOS location permission to display the user's current location on the map and to determine the map view when the user resets the map camera. Users can deny or revoke location permission at any time. Denying location permission does not affect the use of other major features of the App.

The device's current location is separate from the place records that users create manually. The App does not continuously store the device's real-time location as place records, nor does it provide continuous location tracking.

1.3 Photos

Users can actively select photos through the iOS system-provided PhotosPicker and add them to their place records. Selected photos and related data are stored locally on the user's device for displaying and managing place photos. The App does not actively access photos that users have not selected.

1.4 Notes and Other Place Records

Users can add text notes, select place types, and record visit times for their places. This content is stored locally on the user's device and is used only for the recording and display features provided by the App.

2. How Information Is Stored

The App uses Apple's SwiftData technology to store places, photos, and related records locally on the user's device.

  • The App does not require users to register for an account.
  • The App does not operate its own servers for storing users' locations, photos, or notes.
  • The App does not currently use automatic iCloud / CloudKit synchronization.
  • The App does not automatically upload users' place records to servers operated by the developer.

Device-level system backups, system services, and other storage services actively used by users may process relevant data according to their respective settings and policies.

3. Data Backup and Restoration

The App provides user-initiated data export and restoration features. Users can export their place records and associated photos into a .bubble backup file. The backup includes place coordinates, names, addresses, notes, place types, timestamps, and associated photos.

After exporting, users can choose where to save their backup files. Users are responsible for storing, transferring, and managing backup files outside the App. Users should also keep their backup files secure and avoid sharing them with untrusted third parties.

The App restores data through user-initiated backup file imports and does not provide automatic cloud synchronization.

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Apple Maps and Geocoding Services

The App uses Apple's MapKit and geocoding services for map display, place coordinate resolution, and address retrieval. When using these features, necessary location information or place coordinates may be processed by Apple.

Apple Privacy Policy

4.2 Photon Geocoding Service

Travel+ members have access to an alternative geocoding service. If Apple's geocoding service fails, the App may send the coordinates of the relevant place to Photon to retrieve alternative address information. The standard version does not use Photon as an alternative geocoding service.

Photon Service Website

4.3 Apple App Store and StoreKit

The App uses the Apple App Store and StoreKit to provide membership subscriptions, purchase verification, and purchase restoration. Subscription transactions are processed by Apple. The App does not directly collect or store users' bank card information, payment passwords, or other payment credentials.

Membership entitlements are verified and updated based on transaction and subscription status provided by Apple. Relevant data processing is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy and App Store terms and conditions.

5. Third-Party Analytics and Advertising

The App currently does not integrate any third-party advertising SDKs, user behavior analytics SDKs, or independent crash analytics SDKs. The App does not collect users' advertising identifiers or usage analytics data through such SDKs.

6. Information Sharing and Disclosure

The App does not operate its own servers for collecting and storing users' locations, photos, or notes, and does not actively upload these local records to servers operated by the developer. However, when using features such as Apple Maps, Apple geocoding, Photon alternative geocoding, and the Apple App Store, necessary information may be processed by the corresponding service providers.

Except where required by applicable laws and regulations or necessary to protect the legitimate rights and interests of users and others, the App will not actively disclose users' locally stored place records, photos, or notes to unrelated third parties.

7. Users' Data Management Rights

Users can manage their own places, photos, and notes through the features provided by the App, including viewing, editing, and deleting relevant records. Users can also manage the App's location permission through system settings.

For backup files that users have exported, they can independently decide where to store them, how long to retain them, and whether to delete them. Before uninstalling the App or deleting its data, users are advised to export a backup to avoid losing records that have not yet been backed up.

8. Protection of Minors

The App is not intended to collect personal information from minors. If a guardian believes that a minor has provided personal information to the App without appropriate authorization, they may contact the developer using the contact information provided in this policy.

9. Updates to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the App's features or related services change. Any updated policy will be published on this page, along with a revised effective date. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically to stay informed about the latest privacy practices.
